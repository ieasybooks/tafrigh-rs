# Third-party licenses

The downstream build and packaging code is MIT, under `LICENSE`. The unchanged archives and their SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `native/upstream.json`; archive-relative paths below refer to their extracted contents. Source preparation modifies only the copies in Cargo's `OUT_DIR`.

- `llama-cpp-2` and `llama-cpp-sys-2` 0.1.160 are MIT OR Apache-2.0, copyright Dial AI and contributors, from <https://github.com/utilityai/llama-cpp-rs>. Their registry archives omit the workspace license files; the updater fetches `LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE` from the exact recorded source commit into `native/licenses/`, recording URLs and hashes. Local binding changes redirect raw imports and expose mtmd encoder parameters; native inference is unchanged.
- llama.cpp is MIT, copyright Georgi Gerganov and contributors, from <https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp>. Its license is retained in `llama-cpp-sys-2-0.1.160/llama.cpp/LICENSE` and copied to `native/licenses/LICENSE.llama.cpp`.
- The full upstream archive retains the source headers and license files for third-party components under `llama.cpp/vendor/`, including nlohmann/json (MIT), miniaudio (public domain or MIT-0), stb (public domain or MIT), sheredom headers (Unlicense or MIT), and hashing helpers (xxHash BSD-2-Clause, rotate-bits MIT, SHA-1 and SHA-256 public domain). These are used by mtmd. Other vendored components retain their upstream notices even when not built. The unused ggml source is included unchanged in the archive but never compiled by this crate.
- The linked ggml and backend libraries come from `tafrigh-transcribe`, which carries its own MIT runtime and third-party notices. All Rust dependencies retain their package licenses: bindgen is BSD-3-Clause; cmake, diffy, flate2, tar, sha2, serde_json, enumflags2, thiserror, tempfile and static_assertions are MIT OR Apache-2.0; tracing, tracing-core and tracing-subscriber are MIT; encoding_rs is (Apache-2.0 OR MIT) AND BSD-3-Clause. See Cargo.lock for resolved versions and their upstream packages for full license texts.
- `tests/fixtures/sample1.wav` is Cohere's unmodified Apache-2.0 example; its source, hash and license are in `tests/fixtures/README.md` and `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`. Audar weights are not part of this package.
